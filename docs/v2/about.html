About this guide

A useful local calendar with clear standards.

Kosciusko Community Calendar helps families and neighbors find reliable public events in Warsaw, Winona Lake, Kosciusko County, and nearby northern Indiana communities.

Editorial foundation

Christian-informed values

The guide is shaped by values of truthfulness, human dignity, family, service, stewardship, responsibility, and neighborliness. These values guide how sources are verified, how events are described, and how safety decisions are made.

Editorial decisions are based on an event’s content, age suitability, source credibility, legality, local relevance, and safety. An event is not excluded solely because of the personal identity of its organizers, performers, or attendees.

What the calendar prioritizes

  • Family activities, festivals, markets, concerts, sports, classes, and community celebrations
  • Church events, worship gatherings, service projects, charity events, and faith-based community programs
  • Youth programs, educational events, local arts, civic events, and public-interest activities
  • Clear dates, locations, admission details, age restrictions, and source links

Editorial scope

The calendar is focused on family, faith, service, civic life, education, recreation, and general community events.

Events primarily organized around sexuality or gender-identity advocacy are outside the calendar’s scope, regardless of viewpoint. This topic-based scope applies equally to affirming and opposing advocacy events.

Ordinary public events are not rejected because of the identity of organizers, performers, or attendees.

What is not published

  • Sexually explicit material, pornography, or adult-entertainment content
  • Events promoting illegal activity, scams, malware, phishing, or unsafe sales
  • Hate promotion, targeted harassment, or credible encouragement of violence
  • Private gatherings, unverifiable listings, duplicates, or events outside the coverage area
  • Submissions whose source, date, location, or organizer cannot be reasonably verified

Lawful age-restricted events may be listed only when they meet the content policy and display a clear age label.

Missing-event review

  1. A visitor sends a public event link and basic details.
  2. The link is quarantined and checked for unsafe redirects, malware, phishing, and suspicious files.
  3. The event is classified for age suitability and content risk.
  4. A moderator verifies the organizer, venue, date, location, source, duplicate status, and editorial scope.
  5. Only explicitly approved events enter the calendar and newsletter.

The current prototype uses GitHub issues for controlled testing. A private moderation queue is required before broad anonymous submissions are enabled.

Advertising and sponsorship

Third-party ad networks, behavioral tracking, and affiliate tracking are disabled.

Optional sponsored local event highlights may be enabled later. They must be clearly labeled, kept separate from organic ranking, limited in frequency, hideable by visitors, and approved under the same safety and content standards as every other event.

Corrections and transparency

Event information can change. Visitors should confirm registration, time, price, age restrictions, and availability with the linked source.

Source quality is shown as official, locally reported, or community-listed so visitors can judge how directly the information was obtained.
